# Compose Prose

Treat facts and requirements as load-bearing; style serves them.

## Compose

1. **Lock the contract.** Build a semantic checksum of ideas, actors,
   facts, numbers, tense, modality, scope, chronology, causality, comparison,
   uncertainty, and quotations. Capture audience, stance, voice, form, length,
   and terms. When additions are barred, map every factual assertion and
   commitment to supplied content; let connective prose only order or clarify.
   Gate: every requirement and relationship is represented before styling.
2. **Build the line.** Open with substance. Give paragraphs one movement.
   Prefer exact nouns, strong verbs, declarative syntax, and accurate diction.
   Cut repetition, unsupported glosses, and permission-seeking hedges; keep
   epistemic limits. Close on the strongest final idea, not a recap. Gate:
   every paragraph advances meaning and the contract remains intact.
3. **Compose cadence.** Let short sentences strike, turn, or resolve; medium
   sentences carry thought; and long sentences gather related detail and land
   on the strongest term. Diagnose the silhouette as short (up to 12 words),
   medium (13–20), and long (21+). Above 100 words, use all three and an
   adjacent contrast when voice and form allow. Let paragraphs turn, hand off,
   suspend, or resolve. Keep recurrence that adds pressure, clarity, or
   emphasis. Gate: every conspicuous length and repeated shape has a rhetorical
   job.
4. **Tune by ear.** Read at speaking pace. Repair mechanical transitions,
   breathless clauses, fading endings, and purposeless sameness. Replace stock
   habits: em-dash splicing with deliberate punctuation; stock `not X; Y`
   pivots with direct assertion; filler with substance; canned validation with
   a fitting human tone; meta commentary with prose; decorative symmetry with
   material structure. Preserve real contrasts. Recount length; rerun the
   checksum and any proposition map; delete unmapped assertions or commitments.
   Gate: habits are absent and elements hold.

Honor optional controls when supplied:

- `REGISTER`: `founding_fathers` (civic), `literary_modern` (vivid),
  `cold_steel` (compressed), or `journalistic` (factual)
- `DENSITY`: `lean | standard | dense`
- `HEAT`: `cool | warm | hot`
- `LENGTH`: `micro | short | medium | long`

Controls tune the voice. Infer neutral, exact register when absent. Return
plain prose unless headings or bullets are requested.
